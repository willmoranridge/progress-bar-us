Recommended actions
1Keep the TECH progress bar live. Begin planning a rollout path — the signal is strong enough to act on. Consider whether the free Ridge Wallet GWP can be applied more broadly across other segments.
2Keep the RINGS progress bar live for another 7–10 days. If confidence plateaus below 85% across metrics, reduce the GWP threshold from $225 to $200 and re-evaluate. The direction is right; the bar may just be set slightly too high for the RINGS buyer.
